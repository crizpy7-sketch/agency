Critic findings

major audio fixed

The game was completely silent

The sfx kit existed but no songs had ever been written. Nine original songs now cover every map and scene, and the regression asserts both that they exist and that the sequencer runs real ticks without throwing.

blocker process

All five builder subagents were killed mid-flight

The account hit its monthly spend limit, terminating every parallel builder. Their partial output was salvaged, verified, and completed directly in the main session.

major art fixed

Every Guardian shares one egg silhouette

Six body archetypes now change the actual proportions — squat Terranox, upright birds with a visible chest, legless tapering wisps, wide quadrupeds, small sprites, upright drakes.

minor art fixed

Brow shadow read as spectacles

An early pass at more expressive faces put a dark bar above the eyes that looked like glasses on half the roster. Removed.

major battle fixed

Type badges were anonymous colour blocks

A coloured rectangle tells the player nothing. Badges now carry their type name.

major battle fixed

Player HP readout collided with its panel edge

Found by looking at the capture, not the code. Panel re-laid out with the XP bar labelled.

blocker village fixed

Entering build mode directly threw

With no building type the placement validator crashed, and because the first scene was entered before the test API was exposed, that failure blanked the whole harness instead of reporting itself. Both fixed.

major village fixed

Locked rows said 'locked' and nothing else

The model had a reason string all along; the UI was reading the wrong field. Rows now say Needs Village Level 3, or which mission opens them.

minor village fixed

Benefit text spilled off the panel

Now wrapped and clipped to the panel bounds.

minor missions fixed

Header and tabs collided at fixed spacing

'In Emberhollow' overran 'Done'. Both rows are now measured, not evenly spaced.

major harness fixed

The boot veil bled into every screenshot

Captures fired during the 450ms fade, so the loading text sat over the game in every image. The veil now leaves the compositor, and the harness retires it outright.

major world fixed

Grass reads as a flat green plane

Rewritten with 2x2 block mottling plus five blade tufts per tile. A first pass overshot into gravel-looking static with more internal contrast than the path, so it was dialled back: walkable ground must stay calmer than what stands on it.

major ui

Dialogue still runs on the foundation fallback

It works and it wraps correctly, but it has no portraits, no speed control, and no designed frame. The dedicated textbox was one of the builders that was cut off.

minor art

Faces are still one arrangement across the roster

Two dots and a nose on every species. Now that silhouettes differ, per-archetype eye shapes and mouths are the next gain.

minor art

Some evolutions are too close in colour

Aquarabbit and Torrentide read as the same blue at a glance; the mass change carries the evolution but the palette does not help.
